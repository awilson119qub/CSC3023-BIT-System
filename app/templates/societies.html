Arts Society

...

Hello! We are Queen’s Art Society. We are a fun and welcoming society, perfect for those who are passionate about art, be it drawing, painting, sculpting or doodling in the corners of your notebooks, or for those who just want to chill and make friends. There is no need for you to be a Van Gogh level artist to join us and have fun, if you can draw a stick-man, or even if you can’t, you are more than welcome in our society!

Computing Society

...

QCS, or the Queen’s Computing Society, is an academic society which hosts a range of social and academic events aimed primarily at students within EEECS, particularly those within the Computing discipline, but open to all. Our social events include pub crawls, laser quest, regular games nights and, definitively our largest social event of the year, the annual EEECS Formal. Any excuse to eat pizza, have a drink (or two) and have a good time! Our academic events include hackathons, industry talks, networking opportunities, cross-school/society events and more! Anything that gives Students the chance to learn, develop, network and have a great time (and eat more pizza.)

Email: qcs@qub.ac.uk

Instagram: @qcsqub
